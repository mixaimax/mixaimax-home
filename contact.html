FAQ
Can vendors pay to be listed?

No. Ratings and placement come only from sandbox testing results, not payment or advertising.

How often are tools re-reviewed?

Every listed tool goes through a re-check on a 90-day cycle, or sooner after a major release.

Do you cover open-source tools?

Yes. Open-source and self-hosted tools are reviewed with the same checklist as commercial products.

Can I suggest a tool to review?

Yes — use the form and select "Tool recommendation request," including a link to the product.
